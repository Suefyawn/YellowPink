## 0. Headline

The store is technically healthy (Semrush 8 errors on 1,079 pages, zero
real runtime errors, 96% email delivery) and traffic is growing (+22%
visitors month on month). Two things matter far more than the rest:

1. **ChatGPT is the sales channel.** 15 of the last 20 orders landed with
   `utm_source=chatgpt.com` (one more from Perplexity). PostHog classes 72%
   of 30-day visitors as "AI" channel. Google organic is second at 14%.
2. **Google shows most of the catalogue without an "In stock" label.** 222 of
   244 live products are vendor-held (`stock_mode=external`, counter 0) and
   were published as `schema.org/BackOrder`. Branded queries where those
   PDPs rank at position 3–5 ("argivital" 268 impressions, "artibro" 185)
   drew **zero clicks** in 28 days. Fixed this session (see §6).

## 6. Changes made this session

  restated `place_order` from the 31 July body and dropped four in-place
  patches: keep-selling checkout at zero (1020), scoped coupon discounts
  (1050), coupon start dates (1060) and Buy X Get Y (1130). Production had

## 7. Recommended next work, in order

1. **Lean into the AI channel.** ChatGPT cites the brand/guide pages;
   every guide should carry a visible "Shop the picks" block linking the
   PDPs it names (only 15% of visitors reach a PDP today). Add a
   `utm_source=chatgpt.com` segment to the admin analytics page and the
   weekly report so the owner sees it.
2. **COD cancellations from AI traffic.** 4 of the last 7 cancellations
   were ChatGPT COD orders. The WhatsApp confirm/cancel flow
   (`whatsapp_messages`, 0 rows) is built but has never sent: wire the
   Cloud API credentials, or at least turn on the confirmation call script.
3. **Backlinks.** 1 referring domain. Prioritise 5–10 Pakistani
   health/parenting sites from the outreach desk with a concrete free
   resource (the calculators and the medical review board are the assets).
4. **Index the July–August posts.** 47 URLs sit in "discovered/crawled, not
   indexed". Link them from the related, already-indexed posts in the
   same cluster (fertility, PCOS, thyroid) and from the category landing
   copy; resubmit through the indexing cron.
5. **Zero-click page-1 queries.** Test titles/meta for
   `best-gastric-stomach-gas-syrup-in-pakistan` (pos 7, 0 clicks),
   `surbex-z-alternative` (pos 11), `best-azelaic-acid` (pos 10); review
   the 28 unreviewed posts so they carry the reviewer byline.
6. **PDP weight.** Trim the RSC payload (related products and answers
   ship full product rows including descriptions); target <120 KB HTML.
7. **Owner settings.** Enable leaked-password protection in Supabase Auth;
   create a Semrush Position Tracking campaign for the 136 tracked
   keywords if the twice-monthly Routine is not enough.
