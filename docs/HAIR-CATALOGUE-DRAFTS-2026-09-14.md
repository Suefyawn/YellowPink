Everything is written: copy, how-to-use, ingredients, benefits, FAQs, SEO
titles and descriptions, weights, categories.

**Two things are needed from you before any of them can go live.**

1. **A product photo.** Every row has `image_url = NULL`. Product photography
   is copyrighted, so it has to come from the brand or your distributor, not
   from a web search. Ask your supplier for their asset pack, or shoot the
   bottle yourself.
2. **Confirm the price.** Your margin depends on what you pay, which is not
   knowable from here. Provenance for every figure is in the table below.
   Change it in the admin before publishing.

Selection follows the owner directive in `AGENTS.md`: imported only, and
prefer brands the store already sells, because sourcing one more line from an
existing supplier is cheaper than opening a new one.

## What publishing these unlocks

- **The hair quiz gets its missing cleanse step.** It currently has no
  shampoo to recommend, because the store publishes none.
- **The dandruff question can be added.** It was deliberately left out
  because no anti-dandruff product was published. Three of these are
  anti-dandruff, at two different strengths: CeraVe for everyday use, Kerium
  DS as a short intensive course.
- **Hair Care goes from 5 published products to 15**, which is enough depth
  for the `hair-care` collection page to stop looking thin.

## Deliberate omissions

- **Not tagged** into the Niacinamide, Hyaluronic Acid or Collagen
  collections, even though the CeraVe shampoo and the OGX Biotin & Collagen
  line qualify on ingredients. Those pages are written about serums and
  creams, and a shampoo in them would read as padding. Worth revisiting if
  the hair range grows enough to deserve its own ingredient pages.
- **Head & Shoulders** (1,600/mo) was considered and dropped. Mass-market
  pricing, thin margin, and it sits against the imported positioning.
- **Nizoral** (1,300/mo, ketoconazole 2%) is a genuine gap. It is the
  pharmacy-grade answer for dandruff that does not respond to pyrithione
  zinc. Left out only because it is a medicine and the sourcing route is
  different from the cosmetic lines above. Worth asking your pharma
  supplier about.
