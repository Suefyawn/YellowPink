# Competitor Gap Analysis — Yellow Pink (June 2026)

Source: Semrush (PK database), organic research + keyword-gap reports.

## Why Semrush's auto "competitors" report was empty
`yellowpink.pk` is indexed but currently ranks for its target terms mostly at
**positions 55–88** (page 6–9). Semrush needs enough *top-100 keyword overlap*
to compute a competitor set, and our rankings are still too weak/sparse — so the
automated competitor report returns nothing. We therefore identified competitors
by SERP inspection of our core commercial terms, then ran keyword-gap reports.

## Reading the gap data — signal vs. noise
DVAGO ranks for thousands of **prescription-drug "tablet uses" queries**
(nuberol forte, augmentin, flagyl, surbex z, gabica…) and generic terms (coffee,
chocolate, condom, pharmacy). **We don't sell prescription medicines, so those
are NOT real gaps.** Likewise most beauty-side gaps are pure brand-name searches
(j. perfumes, miss rose, sephora, huda beauty, maybelline). Stripping that out
leaves the genuinely relevant, on-catalogue opportunities below.

## Recommended actions, prioritised

1. **Magnesium glycinate (40.5k) — biggest single win.** We literally sell it
   (Calco Fit). Write a dedicated "Magnesium Glycinate in Pakistan" guide and
   strengthen internal links from the existing magnesium-for-sleep post; ensure
   the Calco Fit PDP targets the term. *New post + on-page.*
2. **Folic acid tablets (head terms ~80k combined).** Write "Folic Acid Tablets
   in Pakistan: Benefits, Dosage & Best Picks" targeting the non-PCOS head term,
   linking FOL Chew / F.lium. *New post.*
3. **Glycolic acid (18.1k).** New ingredient guide matching the existing
   salicylic/niacinamide series; link The Ordinary glycolic toner. *New post.*
4. **CeraVe brand push (combined ~64k).** We carry CeraVe — optimise the
   `/brand/cerave` page copy + a "CeraVe in Pakistan" buying guide so we capture
   brand+product demand instead of resellers. *Brand page + post.*
5. **Vitamin E / Evion (22.2k)** and **Centrum/multivitamin comparison** — lower
   priority, brand-led; tackle after 1–4.

## Bottom line
The catalogue and content already cover the **acne / pigmentation / fertility /
PCOS** clusters well. The clearest untapped demand is in **mainstream supplement
head terms we literally stock** — magnesium glycinate, folic acid, vitamin E —
plus **glycolic acid** and the **CeraVe** brand cluster. These are high-volume,
low-to-moderate difficulty, and have direct product fit, so they're the highest-
ROI next content/SEO targets.
