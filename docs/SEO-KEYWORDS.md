## 2. Content gaps (informational → blog)

High-volume informational queries with no current article. Map each to a
`/blog` post that internally links to the matching PDP(s)/category:

- **ashwagandha benefits** (8,100) → "Ashwagandha: benefits, dosage & who it's
  for" → link Trimo-M, Men's Vitality combo.
- **magnesium glycinate** (40,500) / magnesium supplement → "Magnesium
  glycinate vs other forms — why it's the gentle one" → Calco Fit.
- **niacinamide serum** (14,800) → "Niacinamide serum: what it does & how to
  layer it" → Anua, Axis-Y, SKIN1004.
- **folic acid** (22,200) → "Folic acid in pregnancy & PCOS" → Simfolic,
  F.lium, prenatal combo.
- **glycolic acid toner** (1,900) → "Glycolic acid toner guide" → Pixi Glow
  Tonic, The Ordinary 7%.
- **kojic acid cream** (3,600) → "Kojic acid for melasma & dark spots" →
  anti-melasma cream, Medicube kojic mask.
