## Operating playbook

### Weekly cadence (2.5 hrs/week total)

Total budget: 2.5 hrs/week, split into three fixed slots so it never gets skipped. MONDAY (Tanya, 60-75 min) — "send" slot: work the sheet top-down by score band; complete 2-3 self-serve submissions OR send 4-5 personalised pitches (never more; personalisation is the whole strategy). Copy the matching template per angle (editorial / directory / stockist), customise 2-3 lines per contact, log every send in the sheet with a follow-up date (+7 days). THURSDAY (Tanya, 30 min) — "follow-up" slot: send one polite follow-up to anything 7+ days old with no reply (max ONE follow-up per prospect, then mark Dead); update statuses from inbox replies. FRIDAY (Owner, 30-45 min) — "review" slot: answer any replies that need the owner's voice (editors, brand distributors), verify claimed links are actually live (view-source for the href, note dofollow/nofollow), and pick next Monday's batch. FIRST FRIDAY OF EACH MONTH (owner, same slot): re-pull Semrush backlinks_overview for yellowpink.pk, record referring domains + Authority Score in the sheet's monthly log, and prune/re-score dead prospects. Rule of thumb: quality over volume — 5 tailored emails a week beats 40 blasted ones, and at ~40 prospects total the list lasts the full quarter without ever needing mass tactics.

### Prioritisation

Work by score band, not top-down through all ~40 at once. Band A (score 8-10 / high): guaranteed or near-guaranteed links — Tier 1 self-serve submissions (wethrift, skinsort, provenexpert, darazcod, store directories) and Tier 4 stockist requests to distributors of brands we already stock (CeraVe, The Ordinary, Anua, SKIN1004, BOJ, Pixi, NB Sons) — warm because a commercial relationship exists. Do ALL of Band A first: zero rejection risk, builds the baseline profile and Tanya's confidence. Band B (score 5-7 / medium): PK editorial and review sites (reviewit.pk AS 37, brandsynario.com AS 34, health bloggers) — highest authority per link but need a real pitch, so only start once the linkable asset (PCOS/Vitamin-D data piece + free health tools) is live to pitch WITH. Band C (score <5 / low): small PK aggregators and long-shot blogs — filler for weeks when A/B queues are empty; never spend a Monday slot solely on Band C. Within a band, sort by Authority Score descending, but a warm contact (existing supplier, past customer, peer PK store) always jumps a cold one regardless of AS. Re-score monthly: a prospect that ignored pitch + follow-up drops a band; one that replied warmly moves up.

### First two weeks

WEEK 1 (starting Wed 13 Aug 2026) — Band A self-serve first, because these are guaranteed links with zero rejection risk: they lift referring domains immediately, give Tanya reps on the sheet before any real pitching, and establish the baseline profile editors will check before covering us. Day 1-2 (Tanya, ~90 min total): set up the tracking sheet with all ~40 prospects, scores and bands; create/complete Google Business Profile and Bing Places; add the site link to Instagram bio and all brand socials. Day 3-5 (Tanya, ~60 min): submit to wethrift.com (store + active codes), provenexpert.com (free profile), skinsort.com (brand + the CeraVe/Anua/SKIN1004/BOJ/The Ordinary SKUs), darazcod.com, and one PK directory (b2bpakistan.com). Owner (30 min, Friday): final read of the PCOS/fertility data asset (the lead magnet — it lands on the site's strongest ranking cluster) and the vitamin-D piece; confirm both are live with republishable charts, and confirm the free health tools pages are polished, since Week 2 pitches depend on them. WEEK 2 — warm outreach before cold: Tier 4 stockist requests go first because an existing commercial relationship means 30-50% reply rates and high-trust, topically perfect links. Monday (Tanya, 75 min): send stockist/'where to buy' template to 4-5 PK distributors of brands we stock (start with CeraVe, The Ordinary, NB Sons — biggest names, most likely to have stockist pages); log all in sheet. Wednesday (Owner, 30 min): send the two flagship editorial pitches personally — reviewit.pk and brandsynario.com — leading with the PCOS/vitamin-D data story and offering first use with original charts; owner sends these because editors respond better to a founder. Thursday (Tanya, 30 min): follow up any Week 1 submissions not yet live; queue Week 3 batch (remaining stockists + 2 health bloggers). By end of Week 2 the realistic scoreboard: 6-9 links live (all self-serve/profile), 6-7 warm pitches out, 2 editorial pitches in flight. Tier 3 aggregators (jamals.com, plazza.pk, thebrandsshop.com.pk) deliberately wait until Weeks 3-4 — they're low-authority filler and shouldn't consume the scarce early hours.

### KPIs (honest ones)

- Month 1: 8-12 links live, almost all from Band A self-serve submissions and baseline profiles (GBP, Bing Places, PK directories, skinsort, wethrift). Reply rate not meaningful yet — most of these need no reply.
- Month 2: 3-6 NEW links — first stockist 'where to buy' listings (expect 30-50% reply rate, these are warm contacts) and hopefully the first 1-2 editorial placements from the data-asset pitch. Cold editorial reply rate: 10-20% is normal and fine for an unknown PK store; 1 placement from 8-10 pitches is a win, not a failure.
- Month 3: 2-5 new links (the curve flattens as easy wins are exhausted); cumulative target 20-25 referring domains (from today's 2) and Authority Score moving from 2 toward 8-10.
- Ongoing health metrics: 100% of live links verified by hand; 0 paid links; 0 links from the flagged PBN cluster; anchor text mix stays ~80%+ brand/URL anchors; site keeps its clean 0-error audit profile and no Search Console manual actions.
- Honest framing: 25 referring domains in a quarter will NOT beat goldenpearl's 599 — the goal is escaping the ~0-authority trap so strong pages can move off positions 50-70, not winning the category in 90 days.

### Don'ts (protecting the clean profile)

- DON'T buy links, ever — no 'guest post packages', no Fiverr/PBN sellers, no paid niche edits. One paid-link penalty erases the clean profile permanently.
- DON'T touch the known link-farm cluster on IP 195.20.19.178 (drjack.world, bye.fyi, jake.eu) or job-board spam domains, even though competitors have them — they inflate counts, not authority, and carry penalty risk.
- DON'T mass-email. No mail-merge blasts, no scraped lists, no automation tools. Max 4-5 personalised pitches per week; every email names the person and the site. Blasting burns the small PK editorial pool (a handful of sites) forever.
- DON'T over-optimise anchor text. Never request exact-match commercial anchors like 'buy vitamin D supplements Pakistan'. Let editors choose; when asked, give the brand name ('Yellow Pink') or the bare URL. Keep the profile ~80%+ brand/URL anchors.
- DON'T do reciprocal link schemes or 'link exchanges' at scale — a one-off genuine cross-promo with a peer PK store is fine; a pattern of swaps is a footprint.
- DON'T spam comments, forums, or Facebook groups with links.
- DON'T follow up more than once. One pitch + one follow-up, then mark Dead. Nagging PK editors torches the relationship for future assets.
- DON'T overstate health claims to land coverage — the medical assets earn links because they're careful and fully cited (DOIs). Exaggerating a stat risks the credibility the whole campaign rests on.
- DON'T ignore what arrives: check new backlinks in the monthly Semrush pull, and if genuinely toxic paid-looking links appear at volume, note them — but don't reflexively disavow over a handful of random spam links (normal for every site).
